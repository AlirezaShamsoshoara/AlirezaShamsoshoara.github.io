## [AI Daily Paper Summary](/blog/ai-papers/)

I read AI and ML research papers daily and post concise summaries here. Each entry covers the key contributions, methods, and takeaways from recent papers across topics like deep learning, reinforcement learning, NLP, computer vision, and more.

**Latest — October 7, 2026**

1. **Cross-Tokenizer Distillation** — Finds that strict token alignment and a shared top-16 vocabulary outperform broader but conflicting supervision. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.08448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08448)
2. **DuoMatching** — Blends image and video teacher signals to improve few-step video quality without sacrificing motion. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.03543](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03543)
3. **TRACE** — Matches BF16 rollout quality with FP4 MoE training while accelerating rollouts by up to 5.4 times. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.07767](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07767)
4. **CheckerBench** — Shows that coding agents still struggle to synthesize reusable static-analysis checkers from real CVEs. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.07557](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07557)
5. **EVISKILL** — Evolves agent skills from replayable evidence and verifies each edit before it persists. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.05030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05030)
6. **SafeActBench** — Exposes where tool-using agents stop investigating too early or act before establishing evidence. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.07753](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07753)
7. **AutoSciBench** — Generates harder scientific-agent benchmarks by closing shortcuts with solver and judge feedback. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.05140](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05140)
8. **WING** — Distills hand-object interaction from egocentric video into robot policies that transfer across embodiments. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.03607](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03607)
9. **Self-Compensating VLA** — Adapts robot control online to execution errors without task rewards or labels. [arxiv.org/abs/2609.37334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37334)
10. **HuatuoGPT-3** — Uses RL-only domain adaptation to deliver strong medical performance from 20,000 training samples. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.05966](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05966)
