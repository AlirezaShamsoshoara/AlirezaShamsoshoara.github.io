## [AI Daily Paper Summary](/blog/ai-papers/)

I read AI and ML research papers daily and post concise summaries here. Each entry covers the key contributions, methods, and takeaways from recent papers across topics like deep learning, reinforcement learning, NLP, computer vision, and more.

<!-- LATEST_PAPERS_START -->
**Latest — November 14, 2026**

1. **Unbundling Vision Encoding** — Finds that dedicated visual capacity matters more than vision pretraining in compute-matched multimodal models. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.17334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17334)
2. **MixUA** — Separates shared agent lessons from private user memories to improve learning without indiscriminate sharing. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.10439](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10439)
3. **RoboHarness** — Gives coding agents a visual-geometric interface that challenges specialized robot policies on manipulation tasks. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.14018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14018)
4. **JustRUA** — Shows that simpler harness design can substantially improve robot-use agents without training a new policy. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.16009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16009)
5. **TurtLES** — Stress-tests neural turbulence surrogates on industrial-scale 3D meshes, exposing unstable rollouts and lost fine detail. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.09567](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09567)
6. **Humanizer Fact Audit** — Finds that AI-text humanizers frequently damage facts, while detector evasion says little about factual fidelity. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.00359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00359)
7. **UAV Consensus Capture** — Reveals how moving traffic can fool video stabilization and shows that object masking prevents the tested failure. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.17190](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17190)
8. **PixVid** — Replaces latent video decoding with a pixel-rendering head to support higher resolution and action-responsive generation. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.17336](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17336)
9. **Experience-Handoff RL** — Trains agents to write playbooks that improve future rollouts, making reusable experience part of the learning objective. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.16409](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16409)
10. **HiVe-Mem** — Combines broader multimodal memory retrieval with compact, query-specific evidence to improve performance while cutting token use. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.12872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12872)
