4. **PROLA** — Splits low-rank updates between principal and orthogonal directions to improve high-resolution weather forecasting. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.11318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11318)
5. **Game2Player** — Co-develops game environments and players to scale verified training experience for visual agents. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.24154](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24154)
6. **HINT-WAM** — Uses privileged future video to teach robotic action models through on-policy self-distillation without simulator interaction. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.24789](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24789)
7. **CurvDelta** — Restricts model updates to low-curvature directions to balance new learning with capability preservation. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.24654](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24654)
8. **LightTAR** — Adds inexpensive target-aware features to multimodal tabular pipelines without changing encoders or downstream learners. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.17600](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17600)
9. **WorldRuler** — Couples executable rules with video generation and feedback to keep interactive worlds configurable and persistent. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.24869](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24869)
