## [AI Daily Paper Summary](/blog/ai-papers/)

I read AI and ML research papers daily and post concise summaries here. Each entry covers the key contributions, methods, and takeaways from recent papers across topics like deep learning, reinforcement learning, NLP, computer vision, and more.

**Latest — November 7, 2026**

1. **Cognitive Symbionts** — Reframes personal AI as a lifelong contextual partner for human thinking, with privacy and trust central to the design. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.05376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05376)
2. **Reasoning History Compaction** — Tests whether compact state records can shorten an agent's future reasoning without retraining, with savings dependent on the model and compressor. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.03852](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03852)
3. **TB-MathModel** — Makes end-to-end mathematical modeling verifiable through numerical scoring, feasibility checks, and formal proof obligations. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.08237](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08237)
4. **HeLM** — Gives robot manipulation policies explicit language memory to retain task-relevant history across observations and task boundaries. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.02660](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02660)
5. **NitroFusion2** — Uses adversarial supervision on diffusion models' text streams to improve typography and detail in one- to two-step image generation. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.05648](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05648)
6. **SwarmEvolve** — Combines latent reasoning, shared caches, and diversity filtering to make large agent swarms practical for scientific search. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.08483](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08483)
7. **SAE Interprets MoE** — Reveals fine-grained expert specialization by tracing interpretable features to expert contributions rather than router selections alone. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.08089](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08089)
8. **Muir** — Refines linear attention's gating and readout to improve long-context recall while retaining efficient training. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.08376](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08376)
9. **DUMP Tokens** — Adds trainable prefix tokens to reduce representational interference and improve retrieval without changing the embedding model's architecture. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.07298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07298)
10. **LiveFoley** — Adapts visual encoding and audio diffusion for synchronized streaming sound in interactive video and virtual worlds. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.05988](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05988)
