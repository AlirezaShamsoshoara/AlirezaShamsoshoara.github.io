## [AI Daily Paper Summary](/blog/ai-papers/)

I read AI and ML research papers daily and post concise summaries here. Each entry covers the key contributions, methods, and takeaways from recent papers across topics like deep learning, reinforcement learning, NLP, computer vision, and more.

**Latest — August 30, 2026**

1. **AstroPT** — Uses a transformer trained on millions of galaxy images as an astronomical ground-truth testbed for validating when interpretability probes recover real structure in LLMs. [arxiv.org/abs/2608.22614](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22614)
2. **PhysCaP** — A physics-informed code-as-policy agent for active perception in robotic manipulation, inferring latent physical properties that passive vision-language-action policies miss. [arxiv.org/abs/2608.21031](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21031)
3. **Context Allocation Laws** — Causal measurement plus closed-loop orchestration to fix flawed evidence-utilization metrics and suboptimal context-budget allocation in generative search and RAG. [arxiv.org/abs/2608.23252](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23252)
4. **Answer Churn Audit** — A repeat-aware audit showing retrieval-augmented QA can flip answers after an index expansion even with model, prompt, and retrieval policy held fixed. [arxiv.org/abs/2608.22856](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22856)
5. **Generation to Simulation** — A systematic assessment of how far generative world models remain from being true simulators capable of replacing physics, game, and RL environments. [arxiv.org/abs/2608.23070](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23070)
6. **ClawProBench** — Trace-aware agent evaluation that scores a full model-plus-runtime configuration with runtime coverage and frozen workplace-style holdouts, not just final answers. [arxiv.org/abs/2608.22510](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22510)
7. **TorchMorph** — CUDA-accelerated morphological transforms that run inside a GPU training loop, avoiding the expensive device-to-host round trips of CPU-only scipy.ndimage. [arxiv.org/abs/2608.24738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24738)
8. **PTXBench** — A benchmark for adapting LLMs to architecture-specific PTX GPU kernel optimization, measuring correctness and speedup on H100 and B200 across GEMM and attention. [arxiv.org/abs/2608.17379](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17379)
9. **FlavourBench** — An automated LLM benchmark using a versioned culinary system for dense executable ground truth, sidestepping brittle human or model judges. [arxiv.org/abs/2608.20574](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20574)
10. **RIBOSPAN** — A 1.61B-parameter bidirectional RNA foundation model natively pretrained to 10,240 nt for full-length transcript modeling at single-nucleotide resolution. [arxiv.org/abs/2608.22849](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22849)
