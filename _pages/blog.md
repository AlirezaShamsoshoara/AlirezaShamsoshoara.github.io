## [AI Daily Paper Summary](/blog/ai-papers/)

I read AI and ML research papers daily and post concise summaries here. Each entry covers the key contributions, methods, and takeaways from recent papers across topics like deep learning, reinforcement learning, NLP, computer vision, and more.

**Latest — November 13, 2026**

1. **SO-Arena** — Shows that fast System One models with strong aggregate accuracy can still make unreliable decisions. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.15777](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15777)
2. **BirdDreams** — Jointly generates perspective and bird's-eye-view video for a reactive driving world model from a single image. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.14815](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14815)
3. **PhysLoop** — Turns language models' physics knowledge into training data and repairs for self-improving video generators. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.16748](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16748)
4. **G-STAR** — Co-scales token capacity with spatial compression to make autoregressive image generation about 20x faster. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.13004](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13004)
5. **Duo** — Splits reasoning from audiovisual generation in a full-duplex world model built for real-time interaction. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.15131](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15131)
6. **Actor, Judge, or Teacher?** — Finds that weak agent actors can still serve as strong judges and reinforcement-learning teachers. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.17167](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17167)
7. **SimpleHC** — Simplifies hyper-connections to keep their training benefits at 30B MoE scale with little overhead. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.05984](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05984)
8. **Open Vision Banana** — Offers a fully open recipe for turning pretrained image generators into multi-task perception models. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.15844](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15844)
9. **Ropedia Xperience 2** — Releases metric-scale 3D scans and egocentric recordings to scale physical AI environments and behaviors. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.17060](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17060)
10. **Test-time Baking** — Distills cross-view attention into lightweight MLPs for up to 23x faster novel-view rendering. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.17331](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17331)
