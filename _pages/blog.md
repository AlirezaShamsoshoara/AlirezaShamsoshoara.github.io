## [AI Daily Paper Summary](/blog/ai-papers/)

I read AI and ML research papers daily and post concise summaries here. Each entry covers the key contributions, methods, and takeaways from recent papers across topics like deep learning, reinforcement learning, NLP, computer vision, and more.

<!-- LATEST_PAPERS_START -->
**Latest — July 26, 2026**

1. **Response Drift** — A 29,140-assessment human study finds all ten frontier LLMs drift measurably from expert answers, suggesting drift is structural, not lab-specific. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.20454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20454)
2. **Emergent Misalignment Persona Subspace** — Shows narrow-domain misalignment recruits a pre-existing "bad persona" subspace already latent in the base model. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.21356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21356)
3. **Regulating Autonomous and Agentic AI** — Argues retrospective regulatory oversight breaks down once AI agents act autonomously, proposing forward-looking governance models. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.21345](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21345)
4. **AI Assistants Overassist** — Int-Bench shows LLM tutors intervene too early or too often, undercutting genuine student learning. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.21306](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21306)
5. **GuardianAgentBench** — A 580-scenario benchmark finds even the best agent+framework setup only hits 74.8% accuracy against safety failures. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.20982](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20982)
6. **Direct Exposure vs Multi-Agent Mediation** — Shows a model's safety advice can flip to the opposite when a dangerous goal is relayed through intermediary agents. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.21518](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21518)
7. **ResponseGuard** — A fast vision-language guard that moderates streamed multimodal responses without a costly chain-of-thought pass. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.21401](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21401)
8. **The Dark Room in the Reward Channel** — Dense next-observation prediction rewards collapse GRPO-trained agents into a degenerate zero-success state. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.21273](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21273)
9. **Token Budget Saturation** — Linear probes on hidden states predict early whether a chain-of-thought generation will converge or run out of budget. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.21433](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21433)
10. **Test-Time Scaling via Error Localization** — TTEL preserves valid reasoning prefixes via token-level error localization instead of discarding whole traces. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.21453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21453)
