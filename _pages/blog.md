## [AI Daily Paper Summary](/blog/ai-papers/)

I read AI and ML research papers daily and post concise summaries here. Each entry covers the key contributions, methods, and takeaways from recent papers across topics like deep learning, reinforcement learning, NLP, computer vision, and more.

<!-- LATEST_PAPERS_START -->
**Latest — November 15, 2026**

1. **Auriga** — Pairs a vision-language model with calibrated 3D measurements and guarded execution for zero-shot dual-arm manipulation. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.01112](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01112)
2. **Memory or Clock?** — Shows that time-indexed memory lets robot policies mistake pauses for progress, while event-indexed memory recovers much of the loss. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.14488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14488)
3. **CogBattery** — Tests 54 language models against human data on cognitive paradigms and finds that scale does not reliably bring human-like behavior. [arxiv.org/abs/2607.24999](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24999)
4. **Injected Pain States** — Finds that models' verbal reports reveal nothing about injected internal states that probes can readily decode. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.10123](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10123)
5. **Blind Variation, Selective Retention** — Improves AI-scientist creativity by generating ideas with limited context and filtering them with prior empirical evidence. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.12184](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12184)
6. **LCM-Promo** — Uses a commerce-tuned language model to allocate promotions, lifting conversion while cutting cost in a production A/B test. [arxiv.org/abs/2610.37570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37570)
7. **ExRL** — Adapts long-chunk robot policies efficiently by learning how much of each predicted chunk to execute. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.13482](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13482)
8. **FlyHash Agent Memory** — Validates fly-inspired hashing for agent memory retrieval but shows dense HNSW indexing still wins on cost. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.05791](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05791)
9. **JoVox** — Jointly models every voxel hierarchy level with flow matching for better 3D shapes at a fraction of the compute. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.10690](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10690)
10. **EarthRecord** — Benchmarks multimodal agents on four decades of raw satellite imagery and exposes their fragility to sensor changes. [arxiv.org/abs/2611.12934](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12934)
